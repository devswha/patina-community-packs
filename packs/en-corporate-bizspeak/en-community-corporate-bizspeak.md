# Corporate phrasing

These are optional editing preferences, not evidence that an author used AI.
Apply them only when the replacement preserves the original claim and voice.

### 1. Abstract use verbs

**Watch words:** leverage, utilize

**Fire condition:** the verb simply means using an existing tool or resource,
and the business context adds no distinct meaning to the word.

**Exclusions:** financial leverage, physical leverage, quotations, product names,
code, and technical terminology. Preserve an intentional specialist term.

**Semantic Risk:** LOW

**Preservation note:** replace only the verb with "use". Keep the tool, actor,
purpose, timing, quantities, certainty and causal relationship unchanged.

**Before:**
> We will leverage the existing dashboard to track weekly sign-ups.

**After:**
> We will use the existing dashboard to track weekly sign-ups.

### 2. Routine follow-up phrasing

**Watch words:** circle back

**Fire condition:** the phrase describes a routine later discussion and "follow
up" expresses the same commitment.

**Exclusions:** quoted speech, a physical return, or an intentionally casual
idiom that contributes to the writer's voice. Do not change a promised date.

**Semantic Risk:** LOW

**Preservation note:** replace only "circle back" with "follow up". Preserve
who will act, when, and what the follow-up concerns. Do not add a decision.

**Before:**
> The team will circle back on Friday to discuss the launch.

**After:**
> The team will follow up on Friday to discuss the launch.
